# AVOS staff guide · Meadows Varkala

One page per desk. Everyone signs in at **https://app.shajianandan.com** with their email and password, on a phone or a computer. The first screen is always **My work**: what is on your desk right now, oldest first, with how long each item has waited and how long it may wait. Nothing on it is typed twice; it is what the books already know.

Three habits that make everything else work:

1. **Open My work when you start, after lunch and before you leave.** Clear it top to bottom.
2. **Record things when they happen, not at the end of the day.** A check-in, a payment, a bill, a payout.
3. **If something waits past its time, it moves up one desk** (sales → revenue, everyone else → owner) and that person is notified. The owner sees everything that slipped and for how long. Nobody is shouted at; the point is that nothing is lost.

## Sales desk · Prachi

**Your screens:** My work, Leads, Inbox — bookings, Reservations, Availability, Guest IDs (to file Form C).

**What is on your My work**
| Item | Allowed wait | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Email from STAAH / SwiftBook waiting | 2 h | Open Inbox, press Book or Dismiss |
| Enquiry unanswered | 1 h | Open Leads, Mark contacted, quote |
| Form C to file (foreign guest) | 24 h from arrival | Open Guest IDs, file on the FRRO portal, record the number |

### Leads
- New enquiries arrive on their own from NeoDove (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, website). A phone or walk-in enquiry you add yourself with **+ Lead**: three taps and a name. Type the campaign if the guest says where they saw us.
- **Mark contacted** the moment you reply. The one-hour clock stops there.
- **Quote → WhatsApp**: tick the rooms, the price comes from the tariff card for those dates, and a ready WhatsApp message opens. The quote is valid 48 hours.
- **Hold** blocks the rooms for 48 hours while the guest decides. Expired holds release themselves.
- **Won** turns the lead into a booking. **Lost** needs a reason (dates, price, no reply, expired, other); that reason is how we learn what to fix.

### Inbox — bookings
- STAAH reservations, cancellations and SwiftBook confirmations book themselves when everything lines up. The ones that wait show the reason on the card.
- The card shows the rooms with who holds them; free rooms of the right type are pre-ticked and the rate is filled in. Check the dates and press **Book**. Press **Re-read** if the email was fixed at the source.

### Reservations
- **New booking** runs in three steps: the stay (dates, adults, channel), the rooms, the guest.
- Rooms are tiles. Grey means taken, and the tile says by whom. Tap a free tile to add it at tariff; change the rate on the tile if agreed. The strip below shows room total, GST and gross, and whether the booking approves itself or goes to the revenue desk.
- On-tariff bookings approve themselves. Below tariff goes to Trupti; you will see "awaiting approval" and the booking is provisional until she decides.
- **Amend**: open the booking, press Amend, change rooms, dates, rate, guest or channel, and write a remark saying why. It is audited and the front desk is told. Use this for a room exchange or a wrong OTA mapping.
- Cancel needs a reason. Delete is only for your own unapproved bookings with no payment.

### Form C (foreign guests)
- When the front desk marks a stay as foreign, "Form C to file" lands on your My work with 24 hours from arrival.
- Open it: the passport page, visa and arrival stamp are there. File on the FRRO portal, type the acknowledgement number into the box and press **Mark Form C filed**.

### You cannot
See money beyond a booking's balance, edit an approved booking's amount, see expenses or P&L.

## Front desk and property · Sindhu

**Your screens:** My work, Front desk, Guest IDs, Payments, Guest invoices, Tasks, Expenses & petty cash, Availability. On the phone the **+** button at the bottom right captures an expense, a collection or a task in three taps.

**What is on your My work**
| Item | Allowed wait | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival not checked in | by 18:00 | Front desk → Check in (or No-show) |
| Departure with a balance | by 12:00 | Collect, then Check out |
| Guest still checked in after check-out date | 1 h | Check out or tell sales to extend |
| Checked-in stay with no ID proof | 2 h | Guest IDs → Take photo |
| Open task | 24 h | Tasks → done |
| Gateway receipt not matched to a stay | 24 h | Payments → confirm which stay |

### Check-in
1. Front desk → the arrival's row → **ID proof** (lime). Mark Indian or Foreign national, pick the document, **Take photo**. One photo per document per guest; both sides if the address is on the back. Blurred? Remove and retake.
2. Back on Front desk → **Check in**. Do this when the guest arrives, even at night. A guest who never came is **No-show**.
3. Foreign guests: photograph passport page, visa page and arrival stamp. That is all; sales files Form C.

### Payments
- **Collect at property** lists who owes money today with the balance filled in. Tap **Collect**, choose UPI, cash, card, Airpay link or bank, type the reference if there is one, done. The balance updates on the booking.
- OTA stays (Booking.com, Agoda, MakeMyTrip…) show "OTA collect — nothing to take". Do not collect from them.
- Airpay receipts arrive by email; if one is not matched to a stay it appears here to confirm.
- **Deposit cash / top up petty cash** is a transfer, not an expense.

### Check-out
1. Front desk → **Check out**. If a balance is due the screen says so: Collect first, then **Issue invoice**. The invoice carries GST at the right slab and opens as a PDF.
2. A housekeeping task is created for each room automatically.

### Expenses & petty cash
- Every petty bill: **+** → Expense → amount → category → photo of the bill. Capture first; vendor and bill number can be filled later from the photo.
- Pick the nearest category; "More…" has the full list. Marketing bills get a campaign name.
- The petty cash book carries forward month to month. "Petty cash in hand today" must match the cash in the tin. If it does not, tell the owner the same day.
- Bank-paid bills (electricity, subscriptions, fees) are the owner's to record, not yours.

### Tasks
- Housekeeping, maintenance and anything else with a room or area, a title and a due date. Mark **done** with a photo where it helps. A maintenance bill paid from petty cash can be attached at the same time.

### You cannot
Change a booking's rooms or rate (ask sales), record bank expenses, see P&L or settings.

## Revenue desk · Trupti

**Your screens:** My work, Approvals, OTA payouts, Smart Reconcile, Revenue overview, Marketing scorecard, plus Leads, Inbox, Reservations, Guest IDs, Payments and Guest invoices to see what the desks did. Change log for the audit trail.

**What is on your My work**
| Item | Allowed wait | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Approval pending (below tariff, refund, amendment) | 4 h | Approvals → Approve or Return with a note |
| OTA payout not settled | 7 days after check-out | OTA payouts → tick the stays on the statement, settle |
| Bank statement line unmatched | 7 days | Smart Reconcile → accept or match |
| Anything sales let slip past its time | raised to you | Deal with it or send it back |

### Approvals
- Only exceptions reach you: a rate below tariff, a refund, an amendment that changes money. The card shows the reason and the amount. **Approve** or **Return** with a note. The booking stays provisional until you decide.
- Refunds you approve are then paid by the owner, who records the transfer.

### OTA payouts
1. Pick the channel. **Stays awaiting payout** lists what has checked out and not been settled.
2. Tick the stays on the OTA's statement and press **Compute**. The formula is gross − commission − payment fee − TCS − TDS = expected, to the paisa, using the terms the owner set per channel.
3. Type the payout reference and date, and the amount received if it differs. **Draft settlement**, then **Settle**. Commission and fee are posted once as expenses, TCS and TDS as recoverable credits, and every stay is marked paid.
4. **Payouts land in** IDFC First by default. The three payouts from before AVOS (Agoda 24 Sep, MakeMyTrip 29 Sep, Booking.com 2 Oct) landed in Yes Bank: pick Yes Bank for those.
5. A wrong settlement: **Undo** with a reason removes every row it created.

### Smart Reconcile
1. Choose **Statement of** (IDFC First or Yes Bank), paste the bank statement rows (date, description, debit, credit) and press **Match**.
2. Four tiers propose matches: reference, exact amount, fee-tolerant for gateway receipts, OTA subset. Nothing is written until you **Accept**. Aim for more than 80% matched automatically.
3. You reconcile credits. Debits (bank expenses, refunds) are the owner's or the CA's.
4. Lines for the Yes Bank → IDFC transfers made before AVOS tracked them: mark them noted, do not match them to anything.

### Revenue overview and pre-close
- Revenue by channel, direct share, OTA clearing for the month.
- Before the CA locks a month, the **Pre-close** card lists the exceptions: unreconciled bank lines, part-paid stays, expenses missing bill details, pending approvals, unsettled OTA stays, uninvoiced direct stays, stays not checked out. Clear them, or **Carry exceptions & clear** with a note, then **Approve for close**.

### Marketing scorecard
- Leads by month and source, how fast sales answered, how many booked, revenue against marketing spend, cost per lead and per booking, by campaign. This is how the agency is measured.

### You cannot
Record expenses, lock a month, change settings or see P&L.

## Accountant · Shubham

**Your screens:** My work, Month close, GST filing sheet, Revenue overview, Guest invoices, Smart Reconcile (debits), Change log, Dashboard.

**What is on your My work**
| Item | Allowed wait | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Month still open | by the 5th | Month close → lock once the pre-close is clear |
| GST not filed | by the 15th | GST filing sheet → file → mark filed |

### Month close
1. Revenue desk clears the pre-close exceptions and approves the month for close. You see the same list on Month close.
2. **Lock** the month. From then on nobody, including the owner, can post into it; a correction goes into the current month with a note.
3. The close pack (summary and registers as CSV) is kept for the month and can be downloaded.

### GST filing sheet
- Taxable value by slab (nil up to ₹1,000 per room-night, 5% up to ₹7,500, 18% above), output GST, TCS to accept from the OTAs, TDS for 26AS, credit notes and advances. Everything is computed from the books; nothing is typed in.
- Mark the month **filed** when done.

### Change log
- Every change in AVOS with who, when, before and after. Filter by month; 100 rows at a time.

### You cannot
See guest ID documents, record payments or expenses, change bookings.
